About our Chapter

Wolvesey Chapter No. 6818 is a welcoming Royal Arch Chapter based in historic Winchester, meeting at Winchester Masonic Centre. Our Chapter brings Companions together in friendship, fellowship and the traditions of the Royal Arch.

Meeting four times a year, we provide a place to continue your Masonic journey and enjoy the company of fellow Companions. Whether you would like to learn more about the Chapter or enquire about visiting, we would be pleased to hear from you.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Royal Arch?

The Royal Arch is an important part of Freemasonry in England and offers Master Masons an opportunity to continue their Masonic journey. Learn more at UGLE.

Do I need to be a Freemason to join?

In England, membership of the Royal Arch is open to eligible Master Masons. The Chapter can explain the next steps.

How often do you meet?

Wolvesey Chapter meets four times a year, in January, March, October and December. Exact meeting dates and times will be confirmed by the Chapter.

What happens at a Chapter meeting?

Chapter meetings bring companions together for Royal Arch ceremonies and business, with fellowship often continuing afterwards.
